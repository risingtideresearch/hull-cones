Cone-chain bottom: authoring tool

Keel, plan and trim

Profile: keel and sheer, both authored — drag their points. The faint line between them is the derived edge of the bottom.
Plan. Drag the chine vertices for half-breadth and station. The dashed polyline is the construction chine that sets the cones; the heavy curve is the edge of the bottom — the approximating cubic B-spline those same points control. The topsides are wall-sided: the sheer shares this same plan curve, so they are a vertical cylinder over it and developable whatever the bottom does.

Body plan. Click a frame to select it, drag its two handles. A frame turns red when a seam next to it fails (see the readout above).
