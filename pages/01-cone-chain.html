Conical bottom from a polyline chine

Each chine segment and its mirror meet at an apex on the centreplane. The bottom under that segment is a cone from the apex. One master frame fixes the first cone; every seam is a curve drawn on the previous cone, and the next cone is projected through it.

Seam definition along each ruling, u = 0 at keel and 1 at chine: x = xB + a(1 − u) + b·4u(1 − u). Keel shift is a, mid bulge is b. With both at zero the seam is the transverse plane through the chine vertex. The fold angle is measured between the tangent planes of the two cones along the seam.
