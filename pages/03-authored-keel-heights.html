Conical bottom from a polyline chine — authored keel

Apexes stay on the centreplane, so each cone's keel is the ruling through its apex. The keel is authored as one height per segment; each keel segment pivots about its apex, consecutive segments intersect at the keel vertices, and the seams rake to meet those vertices. Keel drop and seam inner shift are derived, not set.

Keel line j passes through apex Pj and the authored point (xmid,j, hj); for the cylinder segment its slope equals the chine segment's. Keel vertex j+1 is the intersection of lines j and j+1; seam inner shift is that vertex's x minus the chine station x. The master frame is drawn in the inclined plane through the chine vertex and its keel vertex. Seam fold is the angle between the two cones' tangent planes along the seam.
